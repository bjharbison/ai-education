LAYER 01 Raw Materials & Specialty Chemicals
🇯🇵
Shin-Etsu Chemical
Silicon wafers · photoresists
~30% wafer share
World's largest silicon wafer producer. 300mm wafers for DRAM and logic. Also supplies KrF/ArF photoresists critical for DRAM patterning.
🇯🇵
SUMCO
Silicon wafers
~25% wafer share
Second-largest silicon wafer supplier globally. SUMCO + Shin-Etsu together control ~55% of the global wafer market.
🇯🇵
JSR / TOK / Fujifilm
Photoresists · EUV resists
SOLE SOURCE risk
Japan controls ~90% of global photoresist supply. EUV resists (needed for sub-10nm DRAM) are manufactured exclusively in Japan.
🇩🇪
Merck KGaA / BASF
Electronic chemicals · CMP slurries
~40% e-chem share
Germany supplies critical specialty chemicals: CMP slurries for planarization, etch gases, and dielectric precursors used across DRAM fab steps.
🇺🇸
Entegris / Cabot
CMP slurries · filtration
US supplier
Critical CMP slurries for TSV copper polishing, ultrapure filtration systems for fab chemicals. US-based but globally distributed.
🇧🇪
Umicore / Heraeus
Precious metal targets · bonding wire
Sputtering targets (Cu, TaN, Ru) for TSV metallization. Gold and copper bonding wire for die interconnection in packaging.

LAYER 02 Fab Equipment
🇳🇱
ASML
EUV / ArF immersion lithography
SOLE SOURCE — EUVExport controlled to China
Only company in the world that makes EUV lithography machines (~$380M each). DRAM at 1z/1α/1β nodes requires ASML ArF immersion. EUV is export-restricted to China.
🇺🇸
Applied Materials
Deposition · etch · CMP systems
Largest equip vendorExport restricted
CVD/PVD deposition systems for capacitor dielectrics, cell arrays, and BEOL metal. PECVD for TSV oxide liner. Largest semiconductor equipment company by revenue.
🇺🇸
Lam Research
Etch · strip · clean
Export restricted
Plasma etch for DRAM cell patterning and TSV DRIE (Bosch process). Conductor clean systems. Critical for high-aspect-ratio capacitor etch in 1α/1β DRAM.
🇺🇸
KLA Corporation
Metrology · defect inspection
Export restricted
Wafer inspection and defect review for yield control. Critical at every layer — KGD (Known-Good Die) testing for HBM stacks depends on KLA metrology tools.
🇯🇵
Tokyo Electron (TEL)
Coaters · developers · CVD
~20% equip share
Dominant in track systems (coater/developer for photoresist). Also CVD and oxidation furnaces. TEL + ASML + Applied + Lam + KLA represent ~70% of all fab equipment spending.
🇳🇱
BE Semiconductor (Besi)
Die bonding · TCB systems
Key TCB supplier
Thermal Compression Bonding equipment used to stack HBM dies at 250–280°C. Besi is the leading supplier of TCB systems critical for HBM3/3E/4 assembly.

LAYER 03 DRAM Wafer Production & HBM Stacking
🇰🇷
SK Hynix
DRAM fab · HBM stacking · KGD test
~62% HBM share HBM3E / HBM4
Primary supplier for NVIDIA H100/H200/B200. First 12-layer HBM3E (36 GB, 1.2 TB/s) in mass production 2024. Entire 2024–2025 capacity pre-allocated to NVIDIA. Fab locations: Icheon, Cheongju (South Korea); M15X fab in Yongin under construction.
🇰🇷
Samsung Electronics
DRAM fab · HBM stacking
~21% HBM share HBM3E / HBM4 Yield issues 2024
World's largest DRAM producer overall but struggled with HBM3E qualification for NVIDIA in 2024 due to yield and thermal issues. Partnered with AMD for next-gen HBM4. Targeting 2026 hybrid bonding HBM4. Fab: Pyeongtaek, Hwaseong.
🇺🇸
Micron Technology
DRAM fab · HBM3E production
~16% HBM share CHIPS Act recipient HBM3E
Only US-based HBM producer. HBM3E qualified for NVIDIA H200 in 2024; entire 2024 capacity sold out. $200B investment plan for US memory expansion. Idaho HBM fab under construction with CHIPS Act support. Boise, Manassas, Singapore fab sites.

// Critical Chokepoints & Supply Chain Risks

⚠ ASML EUV Monopoly
ASML is the sole manufacturer of EUV lithography tools. Each costs $150–380M and takes 18+ months to build. Loss of ASML supply (geopolitical disruption, factory incident) halts all sub-7nm DRAM production globally within 1–2 years. China cannot access EUV; this is the primary export control chokepoint.
⚠ Taiwan Concentration (TSMC CoWoS)
All NVIDIA H/B-series GPUs are packaged at TSMC's CoWoS facilities in Taiwan. TSMC's CoWoS lines ran at 95% utilisation in 2024 and are the primary bottleneck for AI accelerator supply. Any Taiwan Strait disruption halts global AI GPU production.
⚠ Korea HBM Duopoly
SK Hynix and Samsung together produce ~83% of global HBM. Both are headquartered in South Korea. North Korean missile tests, geopolitical instability, or natural disasters affecting Icheon, Cheongju, or Pyeongtaek fabs would cause immediate global HBM shortage. Lead times already exceed 12 months.
⚡ ABF Substrate — Ajinomoto
Ajinomoto (a Japanese food company) is the sole producer of ABF film, the dielectric used in virtually all advanced IC substrates. A supply disruption would affect GPU packaging timelines within weeks. This is the most underappreciated single-company risk in the semiconductor supply chain.
⚡ Japanese Chemical Monopolies
Japan controls ~90% of photoresist supply, ~70% of silicon wafer supply, and dominates high-purity chemicals. Japan imposed export controls on photoresists and etch gases to Korea in 2019 (since eased), demonstrating how quickly this dependency can become a weapon.
⚡ HBM Demand vs Supply Gap
Global HBM demand exceeded 25M units in 2024 against supply of ~20M units. Lead times stretch beyond 12 months. Micron's entire 2024–2025 HBM3E capacity was pre-sold. Demand is growing at 40%+ annually; supply capacity additions take 2–3 years to build out.
ℹ US Export Controls (BIS)
October 2022 and October 2023 BIS rules restrict HBM exports to China above performance thresholds. December 2024 rules tightened further. Korea separately introduced HBM export controls. CXMT and Huawei/XMC are building domestic alternatives but trail 2–3 generations behind. China's $200B semiconductor subsidy aims to close the gap.
ℹ CHIPS Act & Diversification
Micron's Idaho HBM fab (CHIPS Act funded) will provide US domestic HBM supply by 2027–2028. TSMC Arizona CoWoS capacity (N4, eventually CoWoS) reduces Taiwan packaging concentration slightly. EU Chips Act targets European substrate and materials capability. Full diversification is a decade-long project.
